AT A GLANCE

Built around your choices.

Reps helps you train, track progress and connect with other lifters. Here are the essentials about how your information is handled.

✓

Your workouts are yours

Your workout records, routines and personal training notes are private by default.

✓

You choose what you share

Community posts require an explicit publishing action. They may be seen or saved by other members according to your audience settings.

✓

Health access is optional

Apple Health permissions are requested for features you choose, and Health data is not used for advertising.

✓

You control your account

You can manage your data, change permissions and request account deletion.

01 / INFORMATION

What information we handle

Training information. You can record routines, exercises, sets, weights, reps, training goals, progress, body measurements, workout notes, achievements and workout photos or videos. Much of this data is kept in the app’s local storage. Where optional backup or cross-device sync is enabled, additional copies may be transferred to the chosen cloud service.

Account information. Creating or using a Reps account involves your email address, account identifier, chosen profile information and session records. Authentication is supported by Supabase; secure sign-in credentials are managed by the provider and device. Your email is not shown in Community posts.

Community interactions. If you use Community, Reps handles the posts you choose to publish, media you attach, your selected visibility, follows and follow requests, likes, comments, saved/shared routines, blocks and reports. Other members may be able to view or retain content you choose to share.

Optional Pro subscriptions. Apple processes subscription payments. Reps does not receive payment-card or bank details. The app uses Apple-verified purchase information, subscription status and an opaque Reps account identifier to link Pro access to the Reps login used to subscribe. Restore Purchases checks your Apple account for eligible subscriptions. Manage or cancel subscriptions in App Store account settings.

Technical information. Service providers may process IP addresses, request timestamps, device/browser details, delivery events and security or diagnostic logs as needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot their services.

02 / STORAGE

On-device by default. Cloud when needed.

On your device

Workout history, private training notes, locally saved workout media, app preferences and offline training data are held on your device. Widgets may read limited workout summaries from a shared on-device app container. Device backups or user-created exports may also contain copies.

Connected services

Supabase supports account authentication, Community records and access rules. Cloudflare R2, through authenticated services, supports newer Community media uploads. If you use backup, sync or export options, selected information may be stored by the destination or service you choose.

Optional Private Account Backup. When you confirm Back Up Training Data, Reps uploads a snapshot of routines, workout history, measurements, goals and saved tracker samples to Supabase under your Reps account. Photo and video files are not included. Access is restricted to your account and authorised service administration; this backup is not end-to-end encrypted. New snapshots replace the previous server snapshot, and account deletion removes it. Local recovery copies and exports remain separate.

Optional iCloud Workout Sync uses your private Apple CloudKit database, separately from your Reps email login. Reps uploads a private workout backup and can merge routines and workout history onto devices using the same Apple/iCloud account. Sync runs while Reps is open. Bodyweight, body measurements, heart-rate tracker samples, progress-photo records, and workout photos and videos are excluded from iCloud sync. Turning sync off does not erase existing cloud backups; manage iCloud data through Apple account settings.

03 / COMMUNITY

Choose what you post.

Publishing to Community is an intentional action. A post may include a workout title and results, a shareable routine, caption, selected photo or short video, and any optional data that the publishing preview explicitly offers. Private exercise notes and Apple Health measurements are not automatically included.

Reps supports private/public accounts, follower approval, post visibility controls, reporting and blocking. Community posts are designed to stop appearing in the feed after 24 hours. Expiry of visibility does not necessarily mean immediate permanent deletion from every database, media store, backup or log; deletion jobs and provider retention may affect when files are erased.

Posts may be copied or captured by other users while visible. Removing a post cannot retract screenshots, downloaded media or routines already saved by someone else.

Saved workout moments remain private unless you explicitly publish or otherwise share them.
04 / HEALTH & WATCH

Health integrations are optional.

With your permission, Reps can work with Apple Health for relevant workout, heart-rate, activity, body measurement, recovery and nutrition information supported by the app and your settings. Reps may write workouts and selected body measurements to Health when you enable those features. Health permissions can be changed in Apple Health and device settings.

Apple Watch can display and control supported workouts, exchange workout state with your paired iPhone, and use workout-related heart-rate functionality where explicitly enabled. The iPhone remains the authoritative source for the workout records exchanged with the companion app.

Reps does not sell Apple Health information or use it for advertising. Your private Health information is not automatically published to Community. Any explicitly offered heart-rate sharing must be separately selected and reviewed.

05 / PERMISSIONS

You decide what to connect.

  • Camera and photos: only for media you choose to capture, select, save or publish.
  • Notifications and Live Activities: for rest reminders and workout status on supported iPhone surfaces.
  • Calendar: for optional scheduling, export or management of workout events according to your chosen access.
  • Location: only when you choose a nearby gym search or another location-based action requiring permission.
  • Apple Health and Watch: for enabled fitness data and companion features, subject to platform permissions.
  • External exercise images and imports: requested online content and chosen imported files may involve external providers.

Some features may be unavailable if a permission is denied. You can change most device permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

06 / RETENTION

Keeping and deleting information

Local training information remains on your device until removed through the app, device storage management or app deletion, subject to separate device backups and exported copies. Account information and Community data are kept as needed to operate those services, administer moderation and fulfil applicable obligations.

Published Community posts normally stop appearing after 24 hours. Actual backend/media deletion can occur later and may be subject to cleanup status, operational records, failed uploads and provider backups. A queued offline post may remain on the device until successfully submitted or removed.

You can start account deletion from the app’s privacy/account controls. Where account deletion is completed, the process is designed to remove associated Community media and account records. If media cleanup fails, deletion may pause for recovery instead of claiming success. Local workout records, iCloud backups, Apple Health copies, exported files, third-party copies and certain technical records may require separate deletion. Deleting your Reps account or the app does not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel separately in your App Store subscription settings.

07 / YOUR CHOICES

You stay in control.

You can manage your profile, Community visibility, posts, follower relationships and device permissions; review or export training information; and request account access, correction or deletion. For a privacy request or complaint, contact getrepsapp@gmail.com. We may ask for information needed to verify account ownership — never send passwords or sign-in codes.

Australian users can also learn about privacy complaints through the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner ↗.

08 / SECURITY

Designed with safeguards.

Reps uses encrypted connections, account authentication, access controls, private media storage and device security mechanisms such as Keychain. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please consider the audience before sharing identifiable photos, personal details or health-related material.

Service providers include Supabase ↗ and Cloudflare infrastructure for Community media and web delivery. Providers and their subprocessors may process technical information outside Australia. Support email is handled using email services, which process correspondence and delivery data.

Reps does not sell personal information. The available project documentation does not indicate an advertising or third-party behavioural-tracking SDK in the app.
