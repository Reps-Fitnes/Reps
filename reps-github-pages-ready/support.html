Frequently asked questions

How do I start a workout?

Open Home and select Start Workout, or go to Routines to choose an existing routine or start an empty workout.

Where are my achievements and records?

Go to Progress for achievements, personal records, consistency and workout history.

How do I manage Community content?

Open Community to view Following and Explore. Use the options on a post or member profile for the available reporting and blocking tools.

Where can I learn about data privacy?

Read the Privacy Policy or visit Account & Data.
