Account & Data

Manage your Reps account, your training data and connected permissions.

Delete your Reps account

Open Reps and go to your Profile, then Settings → Privacy & Account. Find the account deletion option and follow the in-app confirmation steps. Account deletion is permanent for the associated online account and Community content. Review the in-app notice before proceeding.

If you cannot access your account or need help locating the option, email getrepsapp@gmail.com.

Manage Apple Health permissions

You can review or revoke Reps permissions in the Apple Health app's data access settings. Reps also provides Apple Health settings for its sync preferences.
